Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Personal Portfolio Website of Kamal Naim

1. Overview & Transparency

This website (kamalnaim.vercel.app) is the personal professional portfolio of Kamal Naim. We believe in transparency and respect for visitor privacy. This page clearly outlines how technical analytics and user interactions are measured across the application.

2. Analytics Services Used

Google Analytics 4 (GA4)

We use Google Analytics 4 to collect aggregated, anonymized metrics such as route navigation events, page views, approximate geographic region (country/city level), browser type, and device screen size. This helps us understand visitor volume, popular technical case studies, and areas where website performance can be improved.

Zero PII Guarantee: Personal data (such as names, email addresses, or form message contents) is strictly excluded from all Google Analytics event parameters.

Microsoft Clarity

We use Microsoft Clarity to capture behavioral metrics, heatmaps, and scroll depth to understand user experience and interface accessibility. All text typed into input fields on the contact form is automatically masked and never transmitted or stored by Microsoft Clarity.

3. Contact Form Inquiries

When you submit an inquiry through the contact form, your name, email address, and message are transmitted securely via EmailJS solely to deliver your message to the recipient's inbox. We do not sell, rent, or distribute contact form submissions to third parties or advertising networks.

4. Cookies & Opt-Out Options

You can disable or manage cookies at any time through your browser settings or by installing the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on .
